### M0 · C5 · Passage 1.0 · Water
**Q1 Matching**
- What **What does your body need?** → You need water to live.
- When **When should you have a drink?** → before you go out
- Where **Where does it get so hot?** → Here in Arizona
- Why **Why does your body need water?** → It helps keep you cool.
- How **How can you tell you need water?** → Your head may hurt.

**Q2 Ordering · Cause and effect** — direction: `Put the steps in order.`
1. When it is hot, you sweat.
2. When the sweat dries on your skin, you cool off.
3. But each time you sweat, you lose some water.
4. So you need to drink water all day.
