## 0. TALL's official positioning (LOCKED Nov 14, 2026)
> **TALL™ teaches teens who can't read yet, whether they grew up speaking English or are just learning it, using real teen topics, a real teacher's voice, and a clear path from first sounds to grade-level reading.**
- Audience: ALL teens (11–17) who can't read yet. English speakers AND English learners. Never a "newcomers only" program.
- English learner support (First 100 phrases, Passage Words, sentence frames) is built in. It is TALL's edge, not a fence.
- Sales line (Marly): "Wilson is for dyslexia. READ 180 is for struggling readers. TALL is for any teen who can't read yet, and it's the one built for English learners too."
- Research behind it (Rhea): all students need explicit letter-sound teaching, whatever their home language; intervention studies show the same results for English learners and native speakers; about two-thirds of 8th graders are not proficient readers.

## ★ 0.5 PACING / ACCELERATION — 20 MINUTES A DAY (must-discuss, Nov 15, 2026 — Miquela)
Miquela: "We don't want a whole year for Level 1 and a whole year for Level 2 — then after 2 years they've only jumped 2 grade levels." And: "Kids do not normally sit for 1 hour to work on their reading. At best 20 minutes."
- Students are already in grades 7–8. **Each level must take weeks, not a school year.** Moving up = mastery (exit check + WCPM goal), not the calendar. Placement lets students skip patterns they already know.
- **Design for 20 minutes a day.** Draft routine (to confirm with research): 2 min sound warm-up · 6 min Word Builder chain · 3 min game or Read list · 2 min Daily Phrase · 7 min fluency (cold read / practice / final read).
- Draft estimate: ~1 word pattern a day → Level 1 ≈ 8–10 weeks if a student needs every pattern; ≈ 4–6 weeks for many students after placement.
- Every screen and activity must fit inside short daily sessions: fast to start, saves progress, no busywork.
- TO DECIDE with research (Credibility Library): weeks per level, weekly plan, test-out rules, district wording ("course" length).

## 0.6 Credibility for EVERY piece (Nov 15, 2026)
- For each part of a level, we must be able to say WHY it helps a teen read better, with research (Credibility Library CL-04+).
- Games must be "valid": the student must hear and decode the whole word to win. Wrong answers differ by ONE sound (never only a final -s). No guessing from the first letter.

## 1. Every question gets a DOK level (Webb's Depth of Knowledge)
- DOK 1 = Recall · DOK 2 = Skill/concept · DOK 3 = Strategic thinking with a sentence frame ("I think ___ because ___. The passage says ___.") · DOK 4 not used in Level 1.
- Level 1 quizzes (14 questions): Q1–10 DOK 1, Q11–12 DOK 2, Q13–14 DOK 3. DOK shows in teacher answer keys and Canvas question names ("Q11 · DOK 2"). Students never see it. (Print keys done in quizzes v3, Nov 15.)

## 2. Passages are nonfiction / informational
- Facts in plain words, checked, sources listed. Question last.
- Two kinds: (a) "how it works / facts" and (b) "two sides of an issue." New sets lean to (a).
- ≥75% First 100 Daily Phrases words, teen topics, ~200–230 words, 3–5 Passage Words.

## 3. Everything flows from the First 100 Daily Phrases
- 44 sounds → clean key word → word lists (46 patterns, every First 100 word included) → phrases → passages.
- Word lists: 10 Build (one-sound-change chain) + 10 Read in every list. ★ marks First 100 words.

## 4. Every piece gets a print version (PDF) when it is built
- TALL Print Style Guide: Montserrat titles + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next reading text, level color, watermark. Student and teacher pages separate. Original layouts only (Six-Minute Solution is a model for WHAT to include, never its look or wording).

## 5. Voice, dignity, ownership
- Miquela's recorded voice only. **No text-to-speech, ever.** If a clip isn't recorded, say "Not recorded yet."
- Teens, never babyish. Diverse kids in every picture, in her ChatGPT cartoon style.
- Original content she owns. No Bridges in anything sold. Watermark everything.
- Out-of-date versions are deleted (trashed), not kept.

## 6. Cold reads are COLD — only the TEACHER unlocks the model read (LOCKED Nov 14, 2026)
1. **Cold Read:** one timed read (auto-stops at 60 s), saved/submitted. No model read anywhere on this step. Re-do only with the teacher PIN reset.
2. **Teacher check → unlock:** the teacher listens, scores, then unlocks the model read for that student and passage with her PIN on the student's screen. Recording, stopping, refreshing, saving, or submitting never unlocks it.
3. **Practice → Final Read:** model read plays as often as needed; then the Final Read is recorded and uploaded to Canvas.
- Applies to every level, app and Canvas. Teacher view can always play it.

## 7. App rules (Claude Code)
- ES5 JavaScript only (var, for loops; no arrow functions, no const/let).
- Every page ends with `</script></body></html>`.
- Test with jsdom click simulation using the real site URL, run the full suite, then push.
- Canvas = Upload (webm), never Record. Fluency scoring is teacher-driven, never machine-guessed.
- Foundations (sounds, word lists, Word Builder) = green #298646. Level 1 = teal #0F766E.
